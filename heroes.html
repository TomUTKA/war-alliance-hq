🧩 Heroes & Factions Overview

Know the hero types.
Know the faction edges.

Heroes are split by type and faction. Understanding range, frontline placement, and faction advantage is the base layer of building good marches.

⚔ Hero Types

  • Guards — Close combat heroes, frontline.
  • Gunners — Medium range, slightly behind Guards.
  • Marksmen — Long range attackers, backline.

🏳️ Factions & Bonuses

  • Rovers (Yellow) — DPS and healing, +20% damage to Stalwarts.
  • Stalwarts (Green) — Defense and buffs, +20% damage to Aeronauts.
  • Aeronauts (Light Blue) — Buffs and balanced, +20% damage to Rovers.
  • Mariners (Blue) — Wild cards, no faction advantage or disadvantage.

📈 Investment Rules

  • Do not grow R/SR skills above level 10.
  • Reserve manuals for innate SSR heroes.
  • Resetting heroes does not return skill manuals.
  • Reset only sends the level back to 1.

⭐ SSR Progression

  • SR heroes can become SSR in seasons, but they still function as weaker purple heroes.
  • Cycle them out for innate gold heroes as soon as possible.
  • Mariner replacements are optional before T10 troops and faction specialist trees.
