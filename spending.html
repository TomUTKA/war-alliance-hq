💸 Low Spender ≈ €/$20 per month

  • 1-Month Pass
  • 4× Weekly Pass
  • Doomsday – choose the hero you are upgrading, not the newest
  • Optional (+€/$10): Hero Weapon Pass

💳 Medium Spender ≈ €/$120 per month

  • Everything from Low Spender tier
  • Daily purchase of “Daily Must-Have” or occasional SSR HERO: 4000–5000% value

💳 Mid–High Spender ≈ €/$150–200 per month

  • Everything from previous tiers
  • Crates of Choice (8 blueprints)
  • Chief Collection Blueprint Pass
  • Situational buys: buy Behemoth / Hero offers only if 3000%+ value

🦾 Big Spender ≈ €/$250–500

  • Everything from previous tiers
  • 10k Skill Book Packs (best value when 10-starring a hero; costs €/$100)
  • Sunday Customized Deals bundle — buy up to €/$20
  • Value Card – Weekly Intel

🐋 Whale ≈ €/$500+

  • Everything from all lower tiers
  • Friday Event – Chief Boost: Purchase until blueprint value is < 1 blueprint per €/$1
  • Special must-buy offers: Behemoth enhancement / upgrade offers until value drops below ≈ €/$20
  • Skill Manual Bundle – 1 purchase
  • Reforge Special Offer – purchase until value is < 1 hammer per €/$0.1
  • Alloy Gear Bundle – purchase until value is lower than 1 armor per 20 €/$
