🏆 Event Guides

Win events with planning.
Not panic.

Condensed notes for Alliance Siege, Turbo Turtle, VS week, and Reservoir Raid. This page is the quick command board version.

⚔ Alliance Siege Event

  • Power used in battle is based on arena power at reset.
  • Adding hero, behemoth, or gear power later does not affect that day’s fights.
  • Unlimited attempts, but only one defeated opponent counts per day.
  • Always try to beat the highest available opponent first.
  • Only hit the bottom player as a last resort so you do not steal better targets from alliance members.

🐢 Turbo Turtle

  • Goal: 5,000,000 points daily.
  • Goal: 3 Race Medals per day, 21 per week.
  • 20 Race Medals = 1 Gold Gear.
  • Do not spend resources in the first 5 minutes after reset.
  • Each day focuses on a different system: troops, construction, research, heroes, chief gear, all-rounder, and all-in push.

🆚 VS Weekly Roadmap

  • Day 1: development — stamina, intel, gear scraps, gathering.
  • Day 2: construction — power plant first, yellow trucks, S-tier missions.
  • Day 3: research & behemoth — finish Alliance Duel, then Special Forces.
  • Day 4: hero evolution — use drafts, fragments, and skill manuals carefully.
  • Day 5: total growth — all speedups and troop training.
  • Day 6: kill event — combat, healing rhythm, and enemy hive pressure.

💧 Reservoir Raid

  • Weekly alliance event, usually Sunday 19:00 UTC.
  • Rostered means attendance is mandatory.
  • Stay in your assigned quadrant and role.
  • Do not use healing speedups — troops return free afterward.
  • Top early priorities: Solar Station, Helipad, Water Treatment Centers, Water Processing Plants.
  • Mid-game priorities: Central Reservoir, Munitions Factory, Dev Compound.
