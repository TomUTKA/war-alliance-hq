⚙ Power Upgrade Guide

Fast, efficient strength.
No wasted upgrades.

These are the core priorities to build march power cleanly and avoid scattering upgrades across weak targets.

🛡 Hero Weapons

  • Upgrade weapons that benefit your whole team composition.
  • Team-wide bonuses beat single-hero bonuses.

🛡 Armor

  • Get full golden armor on every hero in your main march.
  • Focus mainly on 5 full sets.
  • Upgrade evenly: 30 → 40 → 50.
  • Enhance evenly: 1 star across all pieces, then 2, 4, and 6.

📚 Skill Books

  • Focus on one hero at a time.
  • Get all 3 skills to 30 or 35 first.
  • Do not spread books too early.

🐉 Behemoths

  • Unlock new skills every 15 stars.
  • Push one behemoth to each milestone first.
  • Then upgrade more evenly afterward.

📍 Chief Gear Priority

  1. Watch / Timer
  2. Knife
  3. Headlamp / Helmet
  4. Journal / Book
  5. Binoculars
  6. Compass

Priority is based on march power gain.

🔬 Research + Construction

  • Research: Alliance Duel → Special Forces → Troops → Heroes.
  • Ignore troop load if the goal is pure combat power.
  • Power Plant to level 30 ASAP.
  • All 3 golden statues to level 30.

⭐ Hero Stars

  • Main 5 heroes should reach 8 stars first.
  • Then move them to 9★ and 10★.
  • Focus only on your main team first.

🛡 Factions

  • 5 heroes of the same faction gives +25% Hero & Troop HP.
  • Full faction teams become more efficient later in progression.
