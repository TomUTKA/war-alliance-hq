Power Upgrade Guide

Power Guide

Simple priorities for fast, efficient strength. This keeps the full structure from the earlier guide instead of trimming it down.

Home Spending Power Heroes Events Weekly Buildings Rules Comms

🛡️ Hero Weapons

  • Upgrade weapons that benefit your whole team composition.
  • Team-wide bonuses are better than single-hero bonuses.

🏰 Armor

  • Get full golden armor on every hero in your main army / march. Focus mainly on 5 sets of golden armor.
  • Upgrade evenly: 30 → 40 → 50 → etc.
  • Enhance evenly: 1 star across all pieces, then 2, 4 and 6. Major checkpoints are 2, 4 and 6 stars because all unlock a reforge-able buff.

📚 Skill Books

  • Focus on one hero at a time.
  • Get all 3 skills to 30 (or 35) for a huge stat boost.
  • Do not spread books across many heroes early.

🐉 Behemoths

  • Unlock new skills every 15 stars.
  • Focus one behemoth to hit each milestone.
  • Switch to more even upgrades after milestone hits such as 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 stars or level 50 / 100 / 150 / 200.

⚙️ Chief Gear Priority

  • 1. Watch / Timer
  • 2. Knife
  • 3. Headlamp / Helmet
  • 4. Journal / Book
  • 5. Binoculars
  • 6. Compass
  • Tier upgrades are big stat boosts. Priority is based on march power gain.

🔬 Research Priority

  • 1. Alliance Duel
  • 2. Special Forces — after VIP 8, transfer excess gems to stamina for hunting level 30s only after unlocking Random Crate in Infected Conquest Research
  • 3. Troops
  • 4. Heroes
  • Only invest in research that boosts march / combat power. Ignore troop load.

🏗️ Construction

  • Power Plant → level 30 ASAP
  • All 3 golden statues → level 30

⭐ Hero Stars & Factions

  • Main 5 heroes should reach 8 stars first, then 9★ → 10★.
  • Focus only on your main team first.
  • Running 5 heroes of the same faction gives +25% Hero & Troop HP.
  • To get the full counter-faction buff (20% damage), you’ll need 5× of Stalwart, Rover or Aeronaut.

✅ Summary

  • Hit milestones: 3×30 skills, 8★ heroes, tier gear.
  • Focus only on upgrades that directly boost power.
  • Full faction teams give a huge HP advantage.
