📜 Rules & Access

Keep it clean.
Keep it usable.

This server and site are built to help State 590 communicate efficiently. Respect the structure so the whole thing stays worth using.

🛡 Server Rules

  • Be respectful.
  • Use the proper channels.
  • Listen to Leaders, Admins, and King’s Guard.
  • No spam or self-promotion.
  • No piracy, NSFW, or suspicious content unless a proper channel explicitly allows it.
  • Do not share personal information.
  • Keep alliance strategies private.
  • Use common sense.

🛡 Verification

  • Upload a screenshot of your in-game profile.
  • Show player name, state number, and alliance tag.
  • Do not blur in-game name or alliance information.
  • A King’s Guard or Admin will review and grant access.

🌍 Shared State Space

This setup is hosted by WAR but can also be used by the wider state in controlled sections. The goal is order, not clutter.

👑 Moderation

  • Tag staff in the right channel if you need help.
  • Do not spam moderators in DMs.
  • Moderators do not handle private DM disputes between members.
  • If someone is bothering you privately: block them and report to Discord Trust & Safety.
