⚔ Welcome to WAR #590

Welcome. You’ve entered the WAR Alliance HQ – State 590 (Tiles Survive).

This server was built to support both WAR members and the wider state, providing a structured place for communication, coordination, and information sharing.

Whether you're part of WAR or visiting from another alliance, you’re welcome here — just respect the space.

🤝 Introduce Yourself

  • Your in-game name
  • Your alliance / state
  • Your power or level

📜 Before You Begin

This server is hosted and managed by WAR, so structure and rules matter. Following them ensures everyone can use the space properly.

🛡 Rules

  • Be Respectful: Treat all members and visitors with respect. No unnecessary conflict.
  • Keep Profiles Appropriate: Usernames, avatars, and statuses must be suitable for a shared community.
  • No Spamming: Avoid flooding chats or sending repeated messages.
  • Respect Staff Boundaries: Tag King’s Guard when needed — do not spam DMs.
  • No Advertising: No promotions or external links without permission.
  • Protect Privacy: Do not share personal information.
  • No Hate Speech: Discrimination or offensive language will not be tolerated.
  • Avoid Politics & Religion: Keep discussion focused on the game and coordination.
  • No NSFW / Piracy / Suspicious Content: Unless explicitly allowed in designated channels.
  • Use Common Sense: If it disrupts the server, don’t do it.

⚠ Enforcement

Failure to follow rules may result in warnings, mutes, or removal.

👑 King’s Guard

  • Moderators maintain order and keep things running smoothly.
  • If you need help, tag them in the appropriate channel.
  • Do not DM them directly without reason.

📌 Important

  • Moderators do not handle private disputes between members.
  • If someone bothers you in DMs: block them and report to Discord Trust & Safety.

🧾 Verification

  • Upload a screenshot of your in-game profile.
  • It must show your player name, state number, and alliance tag.
  • Do not blur your in-game name or alliance information.
