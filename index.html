Efficient Spending Guide

Simple spending tiers for better value and fewer bad buys.

💸 Low Spender ≈ €/$20 per month

  • 1-Month Pass
  • 4× Weekly Pass
  • Doomsday — choose the hero you are upgrading, not the newest
  • Optional: Hero Weapon Pass

💳 Medium Spender ≈ €/$120 per month

  • Everything from Low Spender tier
  • Daily purchase of Daily Must-Have or occasional SSR Hero 4000–5000% value

💳 Mid–High Spender ≈ €/$150–200 per month

  • Everything from previous tiers
  • Crates of Choice (8 blueprints)
  • Chief Collection Blueprint Pass
  • Situational: Buy Behemoth / Hero offers only if 3000%+ value

🦾 Big Spender ≈ €/$250–500

  • Everything from previous tiers
  • 10k Skill Book Packs when 10-starring a hero
  • Sunday Customized Deals bundle up to €/$20
  • Value Card – Weekly Intel

🐋 Whale ≈ €/$500+

  • Everything from all lower tiers
  • Friday Event – Chief Boost: buy until blueprint value drops below 1 blueprint per €/$1
  • Behemoth enhancement / upgrade offers until value drops below about €/$20
  • Skill Manual Bundle – 1 purchase
  • Reforge Special Offer – buy until value is below 1 hammer per €/$0.1
  • Alloy Gear Bundle – buy until value is lower than 1 armor per €/$20

Power Upgrade Guide

Fast, efficient strength without wasting upgrades.

⚙ Core Priorities

  • Hero Weapons: focus on bonuses that benefit your whole team composition.
  • Skill Books: focus on one hero at a time. Get all 3 skills to 30 or 35 first.
  • Hero Stars: your main 5 heroes should reach 8 stars first, then 9★ and 10★.
  • Factions: 5 heroes of the same faction gives +25% Hero & Troop HP.

🛡 Armor

  • Get full golden armor on every hero in your main army or march.
  • Focus mainly on 5 sets of golden armor.
  • Upgrade evenly: 30 → 40 → 50.
  • Enhance evenly: 1 star across all pieces, then 2, 4, and 6 for reforge-able buffs.

🐉 Behemoths

  • Unlock new skills every 15 stars.
  • Focus one behemoth to hit major milestones first: 15, 30, 45, 60 stars.
  • After milestone hits, upgrade more evenly across behemoths.

🔬 Research + Construction

  • Research priority: Alliance Duel → Special Forces → Troops → Heroes.
  • Ignore troop load research if the goal is pure march/combat power.
  • Construction: Power Plant to level 30 ASAP.
  • All 3 golden statues to level 30.

📍 Chief Gear Priority

  • 1. Watch / Timer
  • 2. Knife
  • 3. Headlamp / Helmet
  • 4. Journal / Book
  • 5. Binoculars
  • 6. Compass
  • Tier upgrades give major power spikes. Priority is based on march power gain.
